# Examples

Copy the pattern, not the words.

## Target voice: a real viral recap (chosen by the user)

The user found this voiceover on a popular Instagram recap and picked it as the standard:

> A 60-year-old grandmother stops a sports car dead in its tracks—by lifting it with one hand. That’s just the start of this wild story about a family of women born with superhuman strength. Years later, Alice, the grandmother’s long-lost granddaughter, arrives in the big city after a life herding sheep in Mongolia. She doesn’t know it yet, but her cracked handprint on the sidewalk and the strange power she carries connect her to a family she’s never met.

What it does:
- **Hook:** an impossible act by a person, with one concrete detail (a 60-year-old, one hand).
- **Frame:** "That's just the start", then the premise in plain words, so you know what the movie is by sentence 2.
- **People:** a role on first mention ("the grandmother's long-lost granddaughter") and one colourful specific (herding sheep in Mongolia).
- **Open loop:** "She doesn't know it yet, but..."

Our Shorts use the same ideas with shorter sentences and simpler words, because a listener on a phone has less patience than a reader.

## Approved: Underwater (2020), 237 words, 59.3 s, reading level 3.6

Four moments, each given time to land, in words a 10-year-old knows.

| # | Sentence | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Norah works at the bottom of the ocean, in a giant underwater base. | Moment 1, the hook: where we are, in plain words |
| 1 | One day, the walls crack, and water starts pouring in. | What happens |
| 2 | Her friends are on the other side of a door, running and screaming for her to wait. | The dilemma, shown |
| 3 | But if she waits, the water floods the whole base, and everyone dies. | The cause and effect said out loud |
| 4 | So Norah closes the door. | The choice, as a punch |
| 5 | She has to live with that. | One line to let it land |
| 6 | And it is only the beginning. | Bridge (frame) |
| 7 | The base is falling apart, and every escape boat is already gone. | Moment 2, the plan: stakes |
| 8 | Their only way out is a crazy plan. | Short promise |
| 9 | They put on heavy diving suits and walk across the dark ocean floor to another base, a mile away. | The plan, concrete |
| 10 | With them is Paul, who escaped in his bathrobe holding a stuffed bunny named Lil Paul. | Moment 3, the human moment: one true, odd detail |
| 11 | He keeps making jokes, even down here. | Why we like him |
| 12 | But they are not alone. | Turn |
| 13 | A creature with no eyes is following their lights. | The danger, named plainly |
| 14 | In a tight tunnel full of water, it grabs Paul and drags him under. | What happens |
| 15 | The last thing he screams is for someone to take his bunny. | The detail that hurts |
| 16 | Norah ends up holding it, and she keeps it with her the whole way. | Let it land |
| 17 | Only three of them make it to the other base. | Moment 4, the cliffhanger: bridge over the skipped events |
| 18 | Above them, the ceiling is covered in these creatures, all asleep. | The big turn |
| 19 | They creep underneath them to the escape boats, but only two boats still work. | The dilemma |
| 20-21 | Three people. Two boats. | Punch |
| 22 | And Norah is the one who has to decide who stays behind. | End hook: the decision, never the answer |

## Rejected

**Too hard to follow** (an earlier Underwater draft):
> At the bottom of the Mariana Trench, Norah shuts a door on her own screaming coworkers, because if she doesn't, the ocean crushes everyone. And that is the easiest choice she makes in this entire movie. Something has torn their drilling station apart, every escape pod is already gone, and the reactor is about to melt.

Why: jargon ("drilling station", "reactor"), a vague "something", and a clever line that needs a second listen.

**Too fast** (simple words, but one new event in every sentence):
> Norah works at the bottom of the ocean, in a giant underwater base. Suddenly, the walls crack and water rushes in. To save everyone else, she has to lock a door on her own screaming friends. Then it gets worse. Every escape boat is already gone, and the whole base is about to blow up.

Why: nothing gets time to land. Pick 3 or 4 moments and stay with each one.

**A vignette with no premise** (the first Underwater video): it opened on Paul and his bunny but never said what the movie is, and it put Paul on the ocean floor in a bathrobe, which is wrong (he wore it inside the base).

**A recap of events** (Life, 2017):
> An engineer breaks quarantine to save him, and Miranda screams that no one goes in. He burns Calvin, and it only grows. Then it slips out of the lab, and the second firewall fails.

Why: events in order with nobody to feel for, roles instead of people, and words like "quarantine" and "firewall".
