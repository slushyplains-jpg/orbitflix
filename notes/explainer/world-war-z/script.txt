One bite turns a neighbor into a killer in seconds. A former United Nations investigator is stuck in traffic while the crowd turns on the cars around him. His wife and daughters are safe on a Navy carrier. But the officers give him one condition. Find where the outbreak began, because only then is there a chance of a vaccine, or lose the only safe place his family has. So they send in a doctor named Fassbach to look for the source. He follows the trail to a South Korean base, where the earliest mention of the word zombie sits in an old memo, and the dead pour out of the dark. In Jerusalem, a wall holds back the horde. Then one loud song draws every infected body toward it. The horde breaks over the top, and the wall gives way. Then a plane goes down, and the cabin shakes with each impact. A mayday call crackles through the radio. Then he learns that these things have a weakness. That changes what he is willing to try. Then word comes in from Cardiff that he is missing. The United Nations wants him back, and a helicopter is inbound. What is he willing to lose to stop this?
