# The Mist (2007): story bible

Sources: SDH track `sdh.srt` (times are H:MM:SS in the film) and the Wikipedia plot summary (cited as "plot summary").

- **Logline:** A father trapped in a supermarket with his young son must protect him while monsters roam a fog that has cut the town off, and a fanatic demands a sacrifice.
- **Protagonist:** David Drayton, an artist and father who lives on a lakeside home in Maine (plot summary).
- **Wound:** Guilt. David argues against sending the grocery bagger Norm out to the loading door (0:24:10 "You guys gonna let this kid risk his life"), and the men send him anyway. Norm is taken (0:26:37 "Hold him still!"). David's failure to stop it is the emotional core.
- **Goal and stakes:** Get his son Billy out of town alive. If he fails, Billy and the others die in the mist.
- **Central mystery or threat:** What is in the mist, and what can it do to the people inside the store.
- **Key beats:**
  1. A storm drops a tree through the window; David's studio deadline is at risk (0:02:02 "extend my deadline", 0:02:22 "tree was gonna come flying through the window").
  2. David drives Billy into town for supplies, and the mist rolls over the lake (plot summary).
  3. In the supermarket, a man screams that something in the mist took John Lee, and the doors are shut (0:12:05 to 0:12:12).
  4. A woman leaves to find her children (0:14:33 "I gotta get home to my kids"; plot summary).
  5. The men send Norm to raise the loading door so the generator can run (0:24:01 "you raise the door").
  6. Norm is grabbed by a tentacle while his friends hold on (0:26:37), and David shouts "Start the generator" (0:27:32).
  7. Giant insects attack the lights (about 0:58 to 1:00, "Are those bugs?"; plot summary).
  8. Mrs. Carmody calls Norm's death the first payment (0:45:02 "Little Normie was first"), and her followers gather.
  9. Her followers call David a Judas and order him to his knees (1:32:04 "Judas!"; 1:30:21 "Get on your knees!").
  10. At dawn, Carmody blocks the exit (1:36:55 "It's dawn"; 1:38:02 "You can't go out. I won't allow it.").
- **Turn:** A soldier admits he is stationed at the Arrowhead base on the mountain (1:30:42 "I'm stationed up there"), and the crowd decides that is why the wrath of God has come (1:30:32). This changes the stakes from a monster to a judgment on everyone inside.
- **Hold back:** The ending. The group runs low on fuel and ammunition, and David ends the lives of the people with him, including Billy, with his last bullets, just before the Army arrives (plot summary; the Army arrives after the mist lifts). Also Carmody is shot by Ollie earlier (plot summary). Do not mention any of this in the script.
- **Best images:**
  1. Grocery bagger Norm pulled toward the loading dock by a tentacle (0:26:37).
  2. The tree crashing through the picture window (0:02:22).
  3. Insects swarming the store lights (about 0:58 to 1:00).
  4. Carmody preaching in the store with the crowd around her (about 0:40 to 0:46).
  5. The soldier with spiders bursting from his body in the pharmacy (plot summary; about 0:41 to 0:42 in SDH).
  6. Dawn outside the store, the car parked at the far end of the lot (1:36:55 to 1:37:16).
- **Cliffhanger:** At dawn, Carmody blocks the doors, and the only car is parked at the far end of the lot (1:36:55 to 1:38:02). The question is whether they can reach it.
