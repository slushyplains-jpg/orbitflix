A storm tears through a quiet town, and a thick mist rolls in off the lake. David Drayton and his son Billy are at home when a dead tree from the neighbor's yard comes crashing through the picture window. David goes to ask the neighbor, Norton, for his insurance information. Soon the power is out, and the whole town is cut off from the rest of the world. Then the mist swallows the streets, and people rush into the supermarket for shelter. Inside, shoppers hear screams outside and see shapes moving in the fog. A man shouts that something in the mist took John Lee. Billy is scared, and he begs his father to go home. Carmody warns everyone not to go out there. Norton tells the crowd to stay calm, while others say they have to get home to their kids. Some say the cloud could be poisonous gas, and the group argues about what to do. Billy cries, and David tries to calm him down. Carmody has her own explanation, and it is not one anyone wants to hear. What is out there in the mist, and will anyone get out alive?
