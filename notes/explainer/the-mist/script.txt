Out of a loading dock, a tentacle yanks a young grocery bagger while his friends grab for his arms. David Drayton is an artist, and his studio deadline is the least of his worries. Earlier, a storm dropped a tree through his picture window, and the tree belonged to his neighbor. He drives his son into town for supplies, and the mist rolls in behind them. Inside the store, he argues with the men who want to send the bagger out. They send him anyway. Outside, a man screams that something in the mist has taken someone. Everyone is told to stay inside, and the crowd shuts the doors. Alone, a woman walks out to find her children, though the crowd warns her not to. Norm is sent out to raise the loading door, so the generator can run. Norm screams while his friends hold on, and David shouts at them to start the generator. Calling Norm the first payment, the preacher demands more. Who is she going to sacrifice next? Some in the crowd call David a traitor and order him onto his knees. Then a soldier admits he is stationed at a secret base up on the mountain. Soon the crowd decides that is why the wrath of heaven has come down on them. At dawn, the preacher says no one may leave, and the only car is parked at the far end of the lot.
