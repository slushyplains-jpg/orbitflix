# Hook options

1. A student has to cut off a man's arm with a dull knife. (an impossible act, a person with a concrete detail)
2. A biology student packs himself in ice so a creature that hunts by heat cannot find him. (wild fact)
3. A man covered in spikes tears through a woman in a gas station bathroom. (shock, but no person to follow yet)

Chosen: 1, because a stranger sees a student with a knife and an arm and has to know why. Hook 2 is used for the last moment.

Model line: But if she waits, the water floods the whole base, and everyone dies.
