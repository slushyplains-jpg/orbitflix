# Hook options

1. A criminal screams as the couple he kidnapped cut off his arm. (an impossible, terrible act with a twist)
2. A man covered in spikes kills a woman, and then her body starts moving by itself. (wild fact)
3. To get past a creature, a man has to make his body colder than the air. (the turn)

Chosen: 1, because the kidnapper and his hostages doing something this horrible together stops a thumb at once, and the next sentences show how they got there. Hook 3 is used for the cliffhanger.

Model line: But if she waits, the water floods the whole base, and everyone dies.
