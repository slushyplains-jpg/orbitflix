# Splinter (2008): story bible

Sources: Wikipedia plot summary (paraphrased), TMDB overview, and the full subtitle digest. Times are SDH times.

- **Premise:** A young couple whose camping trip falls apart is carjacked by two criminals, and all four end up trapped in a lonely gas station by a parasite that takes over dead bodies and hunts by body heat.
- **Protagonist:** Seth, a biology PhD student who is more at home with books about trees than with tents (6:27, 8:29, 14:13), and his girlfriend Polly.
- **Wild facts:** a man covered in spikes kills Lacey in the gas station (24:49, 25:36); her body then moves on its own and goes for the blood (31:33 to 31:45); a tiny splinter infects Dennis, so Seth, a student and not a doctor, cuts off his arm with a dull knife (54:11 to 56:56); the creature hunts only by heat, so Seth packs himself in ice to stay below 93 degrees (1:01:23 to 1:02:19); a sheriff is torn in half (38:57 to 39:33).
- **Human detail:** Dennis once shot a truck driver, and the man's wife went bankrupt paying for his care; Dennis wants his stolen money to go to her ("if I ever got a second chance, she should too") (58:02 to 59:07).
- **Wound:** Dennis and Lacey wanted a baby girl and a clean start in Mexico (36:16 to 36:23); Dennis's guilt over the trucker (58:23).
- **Key beats:**
  1. Seth and Polly's tent fails; they head for a motel (7:08 to 7:31). LOW.
  2. Dennis and Lacey steal their car and take them along (11:01 to 11:52). LOW.
  3. They hit an animal, and Lacey is attacked by it (14:47 to 18:43). LOW.
  4. At the gas station a man covered in spikes kills Lacey (24:49 to 25:36). HIGH.
  5. Lacey's body moves by itself and goes for the blood; Seth says it is like a mold that feeds on blood (31:33 to 32:59). HIGH.
  6. A sheriff arrives and is torn in half (37:28 to 39:33). HIGH.
  7. The plan to burn the forest and call for help with a taped phone line (42:52 to 50:32). LOW.
  8. Dennis gets a splinter in his arm, and Seth cuts it off (54:11 to 56:56). HIGH.
  9. Dennis tells them about the truck driver and his widow (58:02 to 59:07). HIGH.
  10. Seth works out that it hunts by heat; the freezer hides them (1:01:08 to 1:01:46). HIGH.
  11. Seth chills himself below 93 degrees with ice and Polly checks his temperature (1:01:54 to 1:04:17). HIGH.
  12. Seth walks out; Dennis holds the creature back and blows up the station (1:04:46 to 1:15:18). HIGH.
- **Twist we can show:** the creature cannot see in the cold, so the only way out is for a man to make his body colder than the air.
- **Hold back:** the walk to the police car, the fire, Dennis's last stand and the ending with the two survivors.
- **Best images:** the man with spikes in the bathroom; Lacey's body moving; the sheriff torn in half; Dennis's arm and the dull knife; Seth packed in ice; Polly reading the thermometer; the dark gas station.
- **Cliffhanger:** Seth, nearly frozen, has to walk out the door to the police car, and if he warms up even a little the creature will find him.
