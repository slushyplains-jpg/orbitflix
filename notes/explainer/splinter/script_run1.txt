A criminal screams as the couple he kidnapped cut off his arm. It all starts with Seth and Polly, on a camping trip for their anniversary. Their tent breaks, so they head for a motel. Then two criminals, Dennis and Lacey, steal their car and force them to come along. They stop at a lonely gas station to buy water. Inside, a man covered in spikes kills Lacey. Soon her body starts to move by itself, going after their blood. Seth studies biology, and he says it is like a mold that feeds on blood. Now one splinter from it is enough to infect you. And Dennis has one in his arm, and it is spreading, so the arm has to come off. But Seth is still a student, not a doctor, and all they have is a dull knife. Afterwards, Dennis tells them about the worst thing he ever did. He shot a truck driver, and the man's wife lost everything. Now he wants to give her all his money. But the creature is still outside, waiting for them to come out. At last, Seth figures out that it hunts by heat. So to get past it, he has to make his body colder than the air. He packs himself in ice. Too cold, and he could die. Too warm, and the creature finds him. Polly checks his temperature, and it drops to ninety five, then ninety four. And then he has to walk out the door.
