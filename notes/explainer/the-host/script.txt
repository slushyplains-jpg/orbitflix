Years ago, two workers are told to dump dirty formaldehyde, and it runs down a drain and into the Han River. Nobody notices, until something starts living in that water. On a busy afternoon by the river, a little girl named Hyun-seo is snatched while her family screams. Her father, Gang-du, runs a snack stand, and his father is there beside him, while Nam-joo is competing for a gold medal on the TV. Soon the news reports that the American Army has shipped a sample of the virus, and officials confirm the creature is the host of a deadly new virus, while disinfection crews are sent to the river. Then a call comes in from the dark. It's me, Hyun-seo! She is alive, trapped in a sewer so big and so deep that she can't get out. The authorities put her on the deceased list, and officers hold the family back. But the family hears otherwise. So Gang-du pushes past the officers. Hyun-seo has been down there without food, and nobody knows how long she can last. Grandpa says he is coming with him, and the family searches for one place on the map, A3. What will they find when they reach A3?
