## Needs a decision before render
1. Sentence 5: "snatched" and "little girl". The subtitles show the attack and the family calling her name, but not the grab itself, and her age is not stated. Keep the wording only if the picture confirms it (or soften to "is taken").
2. Sentence 6: "already protesting" puts the protest before the sewer call, but the protest line is at 4072. Reword to "and citizens are protesting" to match the timeline.
3. Sentence 11: "no one else is coming" is a summary, not a line. Keep as narration.
