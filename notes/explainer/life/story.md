# Life (2017): story bible

Sources: Wikipedia plot summary (paraphrased) and the full subtitle digest. Times are SDH times.

- **Logline:** The one officer on a space station whose only job is to keep a Martian life-form sealed in has to watch it get out, one barrier at a time.
- **Protagonist:** Miranda, the station's quarantine officer.
- **Wound:** She wrote the containment protocol herself, the three firewalls, and insisted the committee sign it (1:11:10 "That was my protocol. I insisted from the beginning"). Her own rule is what will cut the crew off from Earth.
- **Goal and stakes:** Keep Calvin from ever reaching Earth, even at the cost of the crew (27:19 "The box must stay sealed, the lab must stay sealed, no matter the risk to Calvin"). Failure means all human life is at risk.
- **Central mystery or threat:** How smart is Calvin, and what will it use to get out? It grows, learns and uses tools.
- **Key beats:**
  1. Schoolchildren name the first alien ever found Calvin (15:24).
  2. Miranda's rule: box, lab, station (27:19; 1:10:26).
  3. Hugh, the biologist who woke it, calls these the greatest days of his life (18:23).
  4. Calvin goes dormant; Hugh shocks it awake (27:34, 28:48, 29:47).
  5. It breaks his tool and grips his hand (29:51, 30:01, 31:33).
  6. The engineer breaks quarantine to save him while Miranda screams that no one goes in (31:55 to 32:23).
  7. Fire fails and it grows bigger (36:48, 41:08); it escapes the lab, the second firewall fails (42:12, 42:16).
  8. Distress call dies; the commander goes out to fix it (43:21, 43:53).
  9. Calvin is outside, cracks her suit's cooling; she stays outside so it cannot come in (46:28, 47:35, 50:56).
  10. Thrusters used to blast it off; fuel gone, the station is falling (54:13, 55:10).
  11. Plan to starve it of air (56:34, 56:47).
  12. A ship arrives; one crew member runs to meet it (1:09:38, 1:11:54).
- **Turn:** The ship is not a rescue (1:10:26). It is Earth enforcing Miranda's own protocol.
- **Hold back:** Hugh's death and the pods, Sho's death, David's plan, the lifeboats, the ending with the fishermen.
- **Best images:** the cell waking up under the microscope; Calvin gripping the glove; the sprinklers and fire in the lab; the suit helmet filling with liquid; the Soyuz approaching; the station in the glow over Earth.
- **Cliffhanger:** A ship has come, it is not coming to save them, and Miranda wrote the rule that sent it.
