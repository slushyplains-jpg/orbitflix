Luke hid behind a shelf while his best friend was beaten to death, and he did nothing. Six months later, four old friends hike the mountains of northern Sweden, the trip the dead man always wanted. Then Dom wrecks his knee, and every step is agony. So they leave the trail and cut through the forest, to cut the journey in half. Deep in the trees, they find an elk, gutted and hung high in the branches. No bear could have put it there. Strange symbols are carved into the trunks all around it. When a storm traps them, they shelter in an empty cabin. Upstairs waits a figure made of twigs, with no head and antlers for hands. That night they all wake up screaming, and Luke finds fresh wounds on his chest. One of them is found kneeling in front of the figure, as if he has been praying to it. They follow a path, because a path means people. It leads to an old tent, abandoned, with its owner's shoes still inside. Who walks away from a tent without their shoes? Something huge moves between the trees. Then the blame comes out. Dom calls Luke a coward, who walked out of that shop without a drop of blood on him. In the night, Hutch vanishes into the dark, screaming. And whatever took him has been following them since that cabin.
