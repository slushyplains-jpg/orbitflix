# Hook options

1. Luke hid behind a shelf while his best friend was beaten to death, and he did nothing.
2. Four friends find an elk gutted and hung high in the trees, and no bear could have put it there.
3. They took a shortcut through a Swedish forest, and the forest took them back.

Chosen: 1. It sets the wound and the protagonist in one concrete image, and the rest of the story pays it off.
