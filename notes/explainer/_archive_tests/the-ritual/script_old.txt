Four friends set out on a hiking trip in Sweden to honor their friend Robert, who would have loved this place. They lose their way deep in the forest, and the trip goes wrong quickly. Dom wants out of the woods, but the others keep pushing on. They spend the night in an old house, and something outside is waiting for them. In the morning, Hutch is gone, and the group is sure something took him. Phil insists it was all a nightmare, but the others know better. Gayle's name comes up again and again, as if they are still thinking about everyone they left behind. The group is scared, and they realize they are being hunted. Dom and Luke are separated from the others, and Luke calls out for him in the dark. Strange voices speak in Swedish about a sacrifice. The creatures are called Jotunn, and they hang their victims from the trees. Someone asks whether the body was taken down from the tree. Luke is left facing the creatures alone. The forest keeps growling, and every roar makes the way out seem farther away. Someone promises that the creatures will not kill them, and the forest answers with another roar.
