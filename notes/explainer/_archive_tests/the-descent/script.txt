Sarah wakes up in a hospital calling for her daughter, and Beth has to tell her the little girl is gone. A year later, six women climb into a cave, hoping an adventure will pull Sarah out of her grief. Then she gets stuck in a tunnel so tight she cannot breathe. Beth talks her through it, and they scramble free seconds before the passage caves in. Now the only way back is buried. Their leader, Juno, admits the truth. She told the rescuers about a tourist cave, and this is not that cave. This one has no name. Nobody knows where to look. Deeper in, they find a rusted piton, left by climbers a hundred years ago. And if anyone had made it out, they would have named the place. The youngest of them sees daylight and sprints toward it. It is not daylight. The light is only glowing rock, two miles underground, and she drops into a hole. Her leg breaks, the bone sticking through, and someone has to push it back in. In the tunnel ahead, Sarah sees a man, but the others say the dark is playing tricks. Until something crosses the roof faster than any climber alive. That was no human. Then the screeching starts, and it is circling them. In the chaos, the others are torn from Sarah, and the screaming begins. And when she finds Beth dying in the dark, Beth whispers that Juno did this to her.
