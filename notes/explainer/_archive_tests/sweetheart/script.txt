Jenn buries her dying friend on the beach, and by morning his grave is empty. A trail of blood leads straight into the sea. A week ago, a storm sank her boat, and now she is stranded on a tiny island with almost no water. Every night, something huge crawls out of the ocean to hunt her. Days later, another friend washes ashore already dead, and she uses the body as bait to see it clearly. It has two sets of eyes and a terrible smell. So she ties a hammock high in the trees to watch for it. The creature finds it anyway, and the hammock drops. Jenn stabs it with a sharpened stick, and it bleeds black. She barely gets away. Then a raft washes up with her boyfriend, Lucas, and her friend Mia. But Jenn has a history of lying, and neither of them believes her. When she runs for the raft alone, they tie her up. Her own boyfriend calls her a black cloud and reminds her that he pays for her entire life. Then the creature drags Mia into the water. Nobody calls Jenn a liar now. The next day, she and Lucas load the raft to sail west, where the planes fly. But it hunts them in daylight, which Jenn swore it never did. It rips through the raft and pulls her toward the dark. Now she is alone, writing a letter for whoever finds her body, and it says she has stopped running.
