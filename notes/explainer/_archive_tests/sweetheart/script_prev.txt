Jenn is alone on an island, shouting for help. She searches for water, and the lapping sound never stops. A fire crackles, and she tries to stay warm. Something in the water snarls and chitters in the dark. Jenn says she saw the creature drag a body back into the water. She says there was a family on this island a long time ago. Her friends do not believe her, and Lucas says she is scared. Lucas says they should light a huge fire so they will be seen. Jenn says she is going to get more food. Jenn says she saw a bunch of planes go that way. Then the creature roars in the trees. Jenn runs toward the shore, calling for them to wait. Lucas tells her it is not that he does not believe her. What is really out there, and will anyone be found in time?
