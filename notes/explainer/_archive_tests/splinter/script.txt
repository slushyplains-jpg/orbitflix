A student has to cut off a man's arm with a dull knife. His name is Seth, and all he wanted was a camping trip with his girlfriend, Polly. But their tent falls apart. Two criminals point a gun at them, and steal their car. At a lonely gas station, one of the criminals walks into the dark bathroom alone. Suddenly, a man covered in spikes tears right through her. Her dead body starts to move all by itself. It follows Seth across the floor, going for his blood. Then it gets worse. Next, a police officer arrives, and a creature tears her in half. Now everyone is trapped inside the gas station, with the doors locked. Outside, it waits in the dark, circling the glass. Soon, a tiny splinter from the creature stabs a criminal named Dennis in the arm. It starts to spread, so they tie off the arm, and it has to come off. But Seth is no doctor, and all they have is a dull knife. Dennis screams as they start to cut. On three, they finish the job. Seth figures out the creature hunts by heat. The creature goes straight for the hottest thing it can find, like a hot car engine. So he packs his body in ice. Too cold, and he could die. Too warm, and the creature finds him. Polly watches the thermometer drop. And now Seth has to walk out the door.
