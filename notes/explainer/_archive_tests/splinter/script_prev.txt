A flat tyre leaves Seth and Polly stuck in the woods, and Seth gets a splinter. That splinter is where it all starts, and it begins to change him. Something inside him feeds on blood, and the others can see what it is doing. Someone is killed, and the group is shaken by how fast it happens. The group realizes it is going to kill them too. They talk about setting the forest on fire, but the fire could come right back at them. A hostage standoff with the police adds even more danger. Polly tells Seth not to let it touch him, and Seth asks her to grab the knife. The group hides in a refrigerator, since the creature cannot track them there. Seth has to keep his body temperature below 93 degrees, or the creature will find him. Seth calls out for Polly in the dark, but the forest is silent. They make it out, though nothing is ever quite safe. Seth and Polly keep calling each other's names through the trees, and neither of them wants to stop. Every sound in the forest could be the creature coming back.
