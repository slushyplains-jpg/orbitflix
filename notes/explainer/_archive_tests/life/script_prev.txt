Miranda is the quarantine officer on board the International Space Station. A capsule from Mars carries soil samples that could hold life. The capsule is damaged, and Rory must spacewalk to catch it before it drifts away. Rory reaches the capsule, and the crew cheers, because the sample is safe. Hugh takes the sample into the lab, and the organism moves for the first time. Miranda says Hugh woke it, and the crew starts to watch it grow. The organism is longer than anything they are used to seeing on Earth. It feeds on glucose, and it shares electrical activity like a growing neural network. Hugh says its curiosity outweighs its fear. The crew nicknames the organism Calvin, and Rory tells everyone to stop calling it that. Miranda warns that this is not a controlled experiment. Then a clamp fails, and the air pressure changes around Calvin's box. Calvin goes completely inactive, and Hugh blames himself. What happens when Calvin wakes up again?
