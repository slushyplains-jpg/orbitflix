# Hook options

1. Schoolchildren name the first alien ever found Calvin, and weeks later it grabs a scientist's hand and won't let go.
2. A cell from Mars wakes up on the space station, and one officer swears it will never leave its box.
3. Six astronauts find the first proof of life beyond Earth, and only one of them has a plan for when it gets out.

Chosen: 1. The contrast of the childish name and the hand grip is one concrete picture that raises "how did that happen?". Hook 2 sets up the rule and is used as the second sentence.
