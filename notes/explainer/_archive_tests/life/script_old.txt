A sample from Mars arrives at the space station, and the crew gets to work. Inside is a single-celled organism, and the crew names it Calvin. Calvin grows very fast, and the lab becomes its home. Rory is angry about the way the crew talks about Calvin. Then Calvin escapes its containment, and the station goes into lockdown. Communications with Control have failed, and the crew is on its own. Rory goes into the lab to find Hugh, and an alarm starts blaring. Rory is strangled inside the lab while his crewmates beg someone to open the door. Calvin is searching for a way out, and it is not going to stop. Hugh warns that Calvin knows exactly what it is doing. Calvin grabs Kat's suit, and David can only watch as she disappears inside. Hugh is hurt, and the crew tries to save him with a defibrillator. Miranda and David plan to escape in the lifeboats, but someone has to call for help first, and Sho is moving the station back into stable orbit. No one knows whether Calvin will follow them. Sho keeps the station steady, and the crew radios Soyuz to ask whether anyone can hear them. Kat's distress call must have reached Earth.
