Schoolchildren name the first alien ever found Calvin, and weeks later it grabs a scientist's hand and won't let go. Miranda is the station's quarantine officer, and her rule is simple. The box is her first firewall, the lab is her second, and the station is her third. But Hugh, the biologist who woke it, calls these the greatest days of his life. So when Calvin falls dormant, he shocks it awake. His tool snaps, and Calvin grips his hand. An engineer breaks quarantine to save him, and Miranda screams that no one goes in. He burns Calvin, and it only grows. Then it slips out of the lab, and the second firewall fails. When the distress call dies, the commander walks out to fix it. Calvin is already waiting outside. Its grip cracks her suit's cooling, and she starts to drown in her helmet. She will not let it in, so she stays outside. With no word from Earth, the crew fires the thrusters to blast Calvin off the hull. That works, but the burn leaves the station falling toward Earth. So they decide to starve Calvin of air. Then a ship appears, and one of them runs to meet it, sure it is a rescue. It is not a rescue. Miranda wrote the protocol herself, and it says Earth must push the third firewall into deep space.
