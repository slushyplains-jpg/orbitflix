A man in a bathrobe walks across the floor of the ocean with a stuffed bunny named Lil Paul under his arm. His name is Paul, and he jokes about everything, even when the walls are breaking. Behind him, a drilling station is falling apart, and the only way out is a mile of black seafloor. Rodrigo is already gone, imploded in a cracked helmet. Something with no eyes hunts them by their lights. Then the path shrinks to a pipe so tight they cross it one at a time on a rope. Paul goes last. Norah waits on the far side with the rope in her hands. His voice drops out of the radio. They pull, and he comes through grinning, joking about something in the dark behind him. Then the rope tightens. His line is tangled, and something pulls him under. He shouts for someone to take Lil Paul. Smith cannot hold on, and the thing rips Paul straight out of his suit. Norah is left holding a stuffed bunny. Emily says what none of them want to hear. They drilled too deep, and now the ocean is taking it back.
