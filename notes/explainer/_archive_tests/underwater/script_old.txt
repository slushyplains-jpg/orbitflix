A deep-sea station called Kepler starts to fail, and a pressure breach tears through its corridors. Norah is a crew member who has been working down in the depths for a while. The reactor is unstable, and the crew has to reach the pod bays before it is too late. Captain Smith orders everyone to move, but the submersibles are a no-go, so the survivors have to walk. Emily says she cannot walk, which slows the whole group down. The survivors head for the pipeline. Something unseen is out there in the dark, and it is following them. Emily describes the creature as having no eyes, only a mouth. The team struggles to hold a massive drill in place, and one of them says they cannot hold on. Norah loses sight of the others, and she calls out into the water. The group is rising too fast, and the pressure is getting worse. Norah hears a voice she thinks is Emily's, and she is not sure it is really her. Norah urges Emily to stop, and then she spots someone who is still alive. The only way forward is deeper into the unknown, and every choice could be the last.
