Norah works at the bottom of the ocean, in a giant underwater base. One day, the walls crack, and water starts pouring in. Her friends are on the other side of a door, running and screaming for her to wait. But if she waits, the water floods the whole base, and everyone dies. So Norah closes the door. She has to live with that. And it is only the beginning. The base is falling apart, and every escape boat is already gone. Their only way out is a crazy plan. They put on heavy diving suits and walk across the dark ocean floor to another base, a mile away. With them is Paul, who escaped in his bathrobe holding a stuffed bunny named Lil Paul. He keeps making jokes, even down here. But they are not alone. A creature with no eyes is following their lights. In a tight tunnel full of water, it grabs Paul and drags him under. The last thing he screams is for someone to take his bunny. Norah ends up holding it, and she keeps it with her the whole way. Only three of them make it to the other base. Above them, the ceiling is covered in these creatures, all asleep. They creep underneath them to the escape boats, but only two boats still work. Three people. Two boats. And Norah is the one who has to decide who stays behind.
