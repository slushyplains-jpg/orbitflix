Rob is leaving for Japan, and his friends throw him a going-away party in Manhattan. Hud is filming the night on Jason's camera, and he takes the job seriously. Then Beth shows up, and the fight starts when she tells Rob, "You never even called me." Hud finds out that Rob and Beth slept together, and the whole party is talking about it. Then a blast shakes the building, and nobody knows what it was. Jason runs out into the street, calling for Lily. Hud keeps the camera rolling as Marlena is caught in the chaos. Rob wants to go after Beth, but his friends tell him it is not that simple.
