In a sealed supermarket, an eight-year-old boy makes his father swear the monsters will never get him, no matter what. Not even two days earlier, David Drayton took Billy to town after a storm, leaving his wife at home. Then a man with a bloody nose bursts in, screaming that something in the mist took his friend. The doors are bolted, and Billy cries for his mom. David warns the men not to open the loading door. They do, and a tentacle drags a grocery bagger into the mist. David's neighbor, a lawyer, accuses him of faking it, while a zealot named Carmody preaches that God demands blood. The neighbor walks out tied to a rope, until the men holding it shout to cut it. He never returns. That night, insects swarm the windows, and a predator smashes the glass. By morning, people are dead, and Carmody, who swore it would happen, has followers. A man burned in the attack is dying, so David heads for the pharmacy while Billy begs him to stay. A soldier stuck to a post gasps that it is the military's fault, before spiders burst out of his body. Another soldier admits his base may have torn open a door to another world, and Carmody blames him. Her followers stab him and throw him to the monsters. At dawn, Carmody blocks the door, and her followers shout that they want Billy.
