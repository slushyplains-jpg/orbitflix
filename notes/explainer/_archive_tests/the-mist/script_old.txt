A storm tears through a quiet town, and a thick mist rolls in off the lake. David Drayton and his son Billy are at home when their neighbor Norton's dead tree comes crashing through the window. Norton, the neighbor, only wants to talk about insurance. Then the mist swallows the streets, and people run for the nearest shelter they can find, which is the supermarket. Inside, the shoppers hear screams outside and see shapes moving in the fog. David tells Billy to stay close, because nobody knows what is out there. All anyone can describe clearly are the tentacles, and they seem to come from everywhere. Mrs Carmody, a fanatical neighbor, tells everyone that this is judgment, and that nobody is going to save them. David needs a weapon, so he asks whether anyone has a gun in the store. When the creatures come for the store, the group fights back, and gunfire echoes through the aisles. Mrs Carmody's warnings grow louder, and the people around her begin to believe every word. Outside, the monsters keep roaring, and the mist seems to have no end. Then the shots ring out again, and the line between safety and horror disappears.
