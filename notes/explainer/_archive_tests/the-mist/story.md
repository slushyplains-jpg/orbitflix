# The Mist (2007): story bible

Sources: Wikipedia plot section (read in full, notes below are my own words), TMDB 5876, and the whole of sdh.srt (times as H:MM:SS in the film).

- **Logline:** A father trapped in a besieged Maine supermarket with his eight-year-old son wants to get him home alive, but the monsters in the mist are matched by the fear and zealotry spreading among the people sealed in with them.
- **Protagonist:** David Drayton, a commercial artist and father. The Short follows him and his son Billy. Everyone else is a role (his lawyer neighbor, the store manager, the preacher), except the antagonist Carmody, a religious zealot, always "she" and "her".
- **Wound:** He left his wife at home to take Billy shopping, and Billy cries for her. David has promised to get him back to her (0:47:31) and carries guilt for every warning nobody heeded, since the bagger died after David pleaded against opening the door (0:22:55 to 0:26:37). Billy's request at 1:36:05 is the emotional core.
- **Goal and stakes:** Keep Billy alive and get him home. If David fails, his son dies and the only family he has left is gone.
- **Central mystery or threat:** What is in the mist, where did it come from, and what are the people inside the store willing to do to each other because of it. The human threat (Carmody and her growing flock) rises as fast as the creatures.
- **Key beats (film order):**
  1. Storm, fallen tree, mist on the lake. David takes Billy to town, leaving his wife home (0:01:42, 0:04:01, plot summary).
  2. A bloodied man runs into the store screaming that something in the mist took his friend. Doors are shut, then an earthquake (0:12:02 to 0:14:21). Billy cries for his mother (0:17:20).
  3. David hears something at the loading door and warns against opening it. They open it anyway and a tentacle takes the bagger (0:21:53, 0:23:19, 0:26:37).
  4. David's lawyer neighbor accuses him of faking it, out of an old grudge (0:34:13 to 0:34:41). Carmody preaches that God demands blood (0:43:39 to 0:45:09).
  5. The neighbor leaves with a crowd on a rope; the men holding it have to cut it. He never comes back (0:49:40, 0:54:03, plot summary).
  6. Night: flying insects swarm the lights, a predator smashes the glass, people die and burn (0:58:04 to 1:05:48). Carmody had said it would happen at night (0:45:41) and gains followers (1:12:04).
  7. A burned man is dying, so David leads a group to the pharmacy while Billy begs him to stay (1:09:30, 1:14:10).
  8. At the pharmacy, creatures attack. A soldier stuck to a post says it is their fault, then spiders burst out of him (1:21:15 to 1:21:50).
  9. Back at the store the burned man has died. Carmody's crowd chants for sacrifice (1:26:04, 1:26:51).
  10. Another soldier admits his base may have opened a door to another world. Carmody blames him and the crowd stabs him and throws him out to the monsters (1:31:12 to 1:33:48).
  11. Dawn: Billy asks his father for a promise (1:36:05). David tries to slip out with groceries, Carmody blocks the door and her followers demand the boy (1:37:49 to 1:39:36).
- **Turn:** The cause is human: the army's Arrowhead Project cracked open another world (1:31:12). The turn that matters for the Short is that Carmody now turns scared people into a mob that kills one of their own, so the real danger inside the store becomes the people, and Billy is next.
- **Hold back:** Ollie shoots Carmody (1:40:20), the escape in the Land Cruiser, the wife's body at the house, running out of fuel, David's last four bullets, and the final reversal when the army arrives. Do not mention any of it. The only forward hint is the father's promise in the hook.
- **Best images:** Tentacle dragging the bagger into the mist (0:26:37). Insects crawling the glass in the dark (0:58:04). A rope going taut in the fog (0:52:39 to 0:54:03). The soldier stuck to the post (1:21:15). Spiders bursting out of his body (1:21:50). A crowd with a soldier pinned down, chanting expiation (1:33:36). Billy, "Promise you won't let the monsters get me" (1:36:05). Carmody blocking the glass doors at dawn (1:38:02).
- **Cliffhanger:** At dawn Carmody blocks the door and her followers shout that they want Billy. Open question: how far will they go, and who is willing to stand between the mob and the boy.
