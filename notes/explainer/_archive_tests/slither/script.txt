Grant is a rich man in a small town in South Carolina. One night, he walks into the woods with a young woman. They find a slimy trail on the ground, and a strange creature jumps out. It pushes its way into his body. A day later, his wife Starla sees that his face is swollen. Soon he turns into a monster, and he chases her through the house.

The police chief, Bill, and his men hunt Grant in the woods. They find Brenda, the woman who went missing. Her body is swollen, and the lumps inside her are moving. She cries that she is so hungry. Then the lumps burst out as hundreds of tiny worms. They go for the men's mouths, so everyone covers their face.

By morning, the worms have taken over the town. Kylie, a teenage girl, hides in a bathroom while her family bangs on the door. Their eyes are wrong, and they want to hurt her. She says the worms are in their heads, and they all follow one monster.

That monster is Grant. Bill has a grenade, and he plans to blow Grant up. But Starla walks in first, and talks to Grant alone. She says she will stay with him forever. Is she lying, or does she mean it?
