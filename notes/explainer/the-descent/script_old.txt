Six women go caving on a trip they planned together, and the adventure is supposed to bring them closer. Sarah is still shaken, and the group's first hardship leaves her struggling to breathe. The cave system is bigger than the guidebook suggested, and there is no easy way out. Somewhere in the dark, something starts to growl, and the sound echoes off the stone. Juno, who led them here, is holding the flare, and it is hers. Then Sarah is separated from the group, and her screams for help echo through the tunnels. The creatures, called crawlers, come out to hunt, and the women find themselves trapped in their path. The others have to decide whether to keep searching for Sarah or save themselves. The group argues over whether Sarah can still be saved. In the chaos, the women realize that the cave holds more than they ever imagined. Every choice they make now could decide who gets out alive. Someone promises to get Sarah out of here, and the only light down here is the light they carry. There is no going back now, and every step takes them deeper into a place with no way out.
