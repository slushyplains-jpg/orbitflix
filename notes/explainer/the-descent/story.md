# The Descent (2005): story bible

**Logline:** A grieving mother joins her friends on a caving trip meant to heal her, but their leader has lied about where they are, and something that hunts by sound is waiting in the dark.

**Protagonist:** Sarah, a woman who lost her husband and young daughter in a car crash a year ago.

**Wound:** She woke in hospital calling for her daughter and was told the girl was gone (4:42 to 5:17). She still hears and sees her daughter, and she cannot let the grief go.

**Goal and stakes:** Get out of an uncharted cave alive. If she fails she dies underground where nobody knows to look.

**Central mystery or threat:** What is down here, and who can be trusted? Juno lied about the cave, and Sarah keeps seeing a man nobody believes.

**Key beats:**
1. Sarah wakes in hospital, her daughter is gone (4:42 to 5:17).
2. A year later six friends gather for a caving trip, a gift to Sarah (10:52, 27:49).
3. Sarah is stuck in a tight tunnel and cannot breathe, Beth talks her through (32:03 to 33:52).
4. The tunnel collapses behind them, cutting off the way back (34:07 to 34:50).
5. Juno admits this is not the cave she filed with rescuers, it has no name and nobody has been here (35:34 to 36:23).
6. Beth blames Juno: she said the trip would be good for Sarah (36:38).
7. They find a piton about a hundred years old, so someone was here before (44:59).
8. A flare shows a second entrance, so there may be another way out (46:22 to 47:01).
9. The youngest runs for what she thinks is daylight, falls into a hole, breaks her leg (47:59 to 49:19), her bone must be pushed back in (50:34).
10. Sarah sees a man in the tunnel, the others say it is the dark (52:06 to 53:10). The light was glowing rock, two miles underground (53:25).
11. Something moves across the cave roof faster than any climber, not human (56:15 to 56:25).
12. The creatures circle and attack, the group is scattered (56:37 to 58:23).
13. Sam works out the creatures are blind and hunt by sound (1:12:14). Sarah finds Beth dying, who warns her about Juno (1:14:09 to 1:14:26).

**Turn:** The cave is not empty. The "man" Sarah saw is one of many blind creatures that hunt by sound (56:15, plot summary), and Juno's lie has trapped them with no rescue coming.

**Hold back:** Climax and ending: Juno stabs Beth by accident and flees, Sarah's discovery of the affair and her revenge on Juno, Sam and Rebecca's deaths, and the ambiguous ending where Sarah escapes or does not. Do not reveal any of it.

**Best images:**
- Sarah in a hospital bed calling for her daughter (4:42).
- Sarah stuck in a tunnel, unable to breathe (32:03).
- The tunnel collapsing behind them (34:07).
- A hundred-year-old piton in the rock (44:59).
- A runner chasing fake daylight, then falling (47:59).
- A bone being pushed back in (50:34).
- A figure crossing the cave roof (56:15).
- Beth dying in the dark (1:14:09).

**Cliffhanger:** Beth, dying, whispers that Juno did this to her. What did Juno do (1:14:09 to 1:14:26)?
