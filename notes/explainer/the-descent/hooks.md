# Hooks

1. Sarah wakes up in a hospital calling for her daughter, and Beth has to tell her the little girl is gone.
2. A year after burying her family, Sarah climbs into a cave where nobody will ever find her.
3. The cave Juno promised her friends does not exist on any map.

Pick: 1. It is the wound as one concrete, human image, and it makes the viewer ask what happens to her next. Hook 3 gives away the lie too early.
