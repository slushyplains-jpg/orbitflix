A group of women go caving together in the Boreham caves, and the trip is supposed to bring them closer. Juno leads the way, and the others follow her into the dark. Juno says there is a river about half a mile ahead, and the route follows it to the mouth of the cave. Beth asks whether Juno is sure this is the right way. The cave is pitch black, and someone struggles to light a flare. Then the screams start, and the women cannot see what is coming. The only way out of this chamber is a pipe, and it drops away into the dark. Juno tells them that it is a drop. Sarah, Beth, and the others are told they are up next. Holly calls out to Juno. Sam pokes at something in the dark with a stick. Juno reminds everyone that rule number one is to file a flight plan and stick to it. Juno warned them that it is pitch black when the lights go out. What will they find when they reach the bottom of the drop?
