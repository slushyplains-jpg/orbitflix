# Sweetheart (2019): story bible

Sources: Wikipedia plot summary (paraphrased, 2019 American film), TMDB 463843, and the full SDH subtitle file. Times are SDH times.

- **Logline:** A castaway on a tiny island, whom nobody has ever believed, must survive a sea monster that hunts at night while the two friends who wash ashore refuse to take her warnings seriously.
- **Protagonist:** Jenn, a young woman whose boat sinks in a storm and who washes up on a deserted island.
- **Wound:** Jenn has a history of not being believed. Her boyfriend says her past lies, such as the mugging before the trip, make it hard to trust her, and calls her a black cloud who ruins every trip (55:07, 56:32, 57:06; 1:09:46 "I've struggled with being believed").
- **Goal and stakes:** Stay alive and get off the island, west where the planes fly, and make the others believe the danger. Failing means being torn apart and dragged into a hole in the ocean (58:02).
- **Central mystery or threat:** What is the creature that crawls out of the ocean, digs up the dead and drags its food into a hole at sea (44:46 to 46:27)?
- **Key beats:**
  1. A storm sinks the boat and Jenn washes ashore with a badly hurt friend (1:09:01; 3:12; 4:00).
  2. The friend dies, she buries him deep, and the grave is dug up with blood leading to the sea (46:14; plot summary).
  3. Something huge hunts her at night and she hides for days (44:46; 1:10:10).
  4. A second friend washes ashore dead and she uses the body to see the creature (1:09:18; plot summary).
  5. She sleeps in a hammock in the trees and the creature finds it (39:27 to 40:15; plot summary).
  6. She stabs it with a sharpened stick and it bleeds black (46:27; plot summary).
  7. Her boyfriend Lucas and friend Mia arrive on a raft and do not believe her (43:48; 44:37; 50:24).
  8. She tries to flee alone, and they tie her up (53:04; 54:18).
  9. Lucas calls her a black cloud and says he pays for her life (56:32; 57:42).
  10. The creature drags Mia into the water (58:50).
  11. Jenn and Lucas head west on the raft, the creature attacks in daylight (1:00:29; 1:03:17; 1:05:01).
  12. It tears through the raft and takes Lucas; alone, Jenn writes a letter and decides to stop running (plot summary; 1:09:01 to 1:10:40).
- **Turn:** The creature attacks in daylight, which Jenn swore it never did, so even the raft is no escape.
- **Hold back:** Lucas's fate, the fire ring, the final fight and Jenn carrying the creature's head to the raft.
- **Best images:** the empty grave with the blood trail; the hammock dropping; the white flare over the water (1:05:27); Mia pulled into the dark water (58:50); the raft torn apart in daylight (1:05:01); Jenn writing her letter (1:09:01).
- **Cliffhanger:** Alone on the island, Jenn writes a letter for whoever finds her body, and says she has stopped running.
