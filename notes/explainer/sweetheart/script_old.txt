A little more than a week ago, Jennifer Remming's boat went down in a storm. Her friends Zack and Brad did not survive, and Zack washed ashore already dead. Now Jenn is alone out here, desperate for water, with nothing for miles. She buried her friend deep in the ground, but the creature came anyway. She believes a monster lives in the water, and she tries to warn anyone who will listen. Her note says the same thing, and she admits there is nothing she can do to prove it. Planes pass overhead, and each one gives her a little hope of rescue. Then Lucas turns up, and his story does not quite add up. He says he saw nothing, and Jenn finds herself tied up as the tension grows. She fears she is being left behind to starve. Something moves under the surface, and the creature's hiss echoes across the water. Anyone who gets too close to it does not survive. Jenn has to decide whether to trust Lucas, or to face the monster alone. A flare explodes overhead, and Jenn shouts for someone to come back. The water keeps moving, and she keeps watching the sky.
