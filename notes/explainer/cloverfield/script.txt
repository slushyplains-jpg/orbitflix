The night before he moves to Japan, the head of the Statue of Liberty comes crashing down his street. Earlier that night, Rob was at his own farewell party. Then Beth walks in with another man. They slept together, and Rob never called her. They fight, and she leaves. Minutes later, the whole building shakes and the lights go out. The news reports a tanker overturned by the Statue of Liberty. From the roof, they watch the city explode. Something taller than the buildings walks between them, and a skyscraper falls. The plan is to cross the Brooklyn Bridge and get out. On the bridge, Rob's phone rings. It is Beth, crying, trapped in her apartment, saying she cannot move. Then a giant tail smashes through the cables, and his brother is gone. On a store television, the army fires at the creature, and smaller creatures swarm the soldiers. The whole city is running away from Midtown. Beth lives in Midtown. So while everyone runs from the monster, Rob walks straight toward it. They take the subway tunnels, until something skitters out of the dark and bites their friend. At an army hospital, her eyes start bleeding, and medics rush her away. The last helicopter leaves at six, before they bomb the city. And when Rob finally finds Beth's tower, it is leaning on the building next to it.
