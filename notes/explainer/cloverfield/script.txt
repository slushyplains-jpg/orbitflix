Rob is leaving for Japan, and his friends throw him a going-away party in Manhattan. Hud is filming the night on Jason's camera, and he takes the job seriously. Then Beth shows up at the party, and the fight starts when she tells Rob that he never even called her. Then a blast shakes the building, and nobody knows what it was. Jason runs out into the street, calling for Lily. Hud keeps the camera rolling as Marlena is hurt in the chaos. Rob wants to go after Beth, but his friends tell him it is not that simple. The group heads for the Brooklyn Bridge, and Rob gets Beth on the phone. Then the bridge cables snap, and Jason is lost, and Rob later tells his mother that Jason is dead. A newscaster reports that the bridge was packed with pedestrians. The creature roars over the city, and the army arrives to evacuate everyone. Beth calls from her apartment, where the whole wall has fallen on her, and she begs Rob for help. Rob decides to go after her, and his friends beg him to think it through while the streets fill with soldiers. What will Rob do when he finally reaches Beth?
