Rob is leaving for Japan, and his friends throw him a going-away party in Manhattan. His brother Jason is filming goodbyes, and everyone is having a good time. Earlier, Beth confronted Rob for never calling her. Then the building starts to rumble. Something huge rises out of the harbour near the Statue of Liberty, and the city erupts. Explosions tear through the streets while people scream and run in every direction. Jason runs back into the chaos, and Lily can't stop him. Lily is left holding the group together while the city falls apart around them. Rob gets a call from Beth, who is in her apartment. Rob decides to go after her, and his friends beg him to think it through. The creature roars through the streets, and Hud keeps filming every moment of it. The group finally reaches Beth, but the danger is never far from them. In the chaos, Hud is lost. Marlena is badly hurt, and the group has nowhere safe to go. Rob, Beth, and the others survive long enough to see the military start bombing the creature. Rob's camera tape is all that's left of that night, a record of everything they lost.
