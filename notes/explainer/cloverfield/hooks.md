# Hook options

1. The night before he moves to Japan, the head of the Statue of Liberty comes crashing down his street.
2. While everyone in New York runs away from the monster, one man walks straight toward it.
3. His brother just died on a collapsing bridge, and he is still going back for the girl he never called.

Chosen: 1. The strongest single image, and it sets up the party and Japan in one line. Hook 2 is reused at the turn.
