# Cloverfield (2008): story bible

Sources: Wikipedia plot summary (paraphrased) and the full SDH subtitle file. Times are SDH times.

- **Logline:** On the night of his farewell party, a man whose city is being torn apart by a giant creature turns back into the danger to rescue the woman he never told he loved.
- **Protagonist:** Rob, leaving New York for a job in Japan.
- **Wound:** He slept with his close friend Beth and then never called her (party argument 13:43 to 17:42; 15:50 "Rob and Beth slept together").
- **Goal and stakes:** Reach Beth, trapped and bleeding in her Midtown building, before the military bombs Manhattan. Failing means she dies alone, and so might everyone with him.
- **Central mystery or threat:** What is the creature, and what are the smaller things falling off it?
- **Key beats:**
  1. Surprise farewell party for Rob, moving to Japan (9:51; 5:44).
  2. Beth arrives with another man; she and Rob argue and she leaves (11:54 to 14:23).
  3. A quake and blackout; news of a tanker capsized near the Statue of Liberty (18:28 to 19:05).
  4. Explosion seen from the roof; they flee; the statue's head lands in the street (19:55 to 22:20).
  5. A creature collapses a skyscraper; dust everywhere (22:35 to 23:49 "It walked right over my head").
  6. Escape over the Brooklyn Bridge; Beth calls, she can't move (27:05 to 27:35).
  7. The creature destroys the bridge; Rob's brother Jason is killed (28:05 to 28:48).
  8. Electronics store; news of the army fighting it; Beth's message, "I'm bleeding, I can't move" (32:11 to 33:06).
  9. Rob heads for Midtown anyway (33:40 to 34:40).
  10. Subway tunnels; small creatures attack; Marlena is bitten (41:14 to 47:22).
  11. Field hospital; Marlena taken away, her eyes bleeding (50:33 to 53:44); last helicopter before the bombing (plot summary; 1:03:41 "6:00, Hud").
  12. Beth's building is leaning against the next tower (54:39 to 54:45).
- **Turn:** Beth's voicemail; instead of escaping, Rob walks toward the creature.
- **Hold back:** the rescue of Beth, the helicopter crash, Hud's death, the ending in Central Park.
- **Best images:** the statue's head in the street; the skyscraper collapsing; the bridge breaking; the creature between buildings on the news; the dark subway tunnel; the leaning tower.
- **Cliffhanger:** Beth's tower is leaning on the one beside it, the last helicopter leaves at six, and Rob is going up anyway.
