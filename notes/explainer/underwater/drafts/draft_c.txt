The core of the Kepler will melt in thirty minutes, and the only way out is a mile of black seafloor. Every escape pod has already left. The captain needs six good helmets, and the first ones Norah checks are ripped. They go anyway. Rodrigo's helmet fails first, and he implodes. Then the station behind them explodes, right on time. Now something with no eyes follows their lights. Smith breathes poison from a damaged scrubber, so every step is a clock of its own. Paul is dragged out of his suit in a pipe. The captain tells Norah to let go, and she says she cannot. Alone, Norah has air, a radio, and no one answering. She finds Emily and Smith alive. They reach the last station with the alarms screaming that oxygen levels are critical. A nest of creatures hangs from the ceiling. At the pod deck, the computer says one pod is broken. Norah tells them there are three.
