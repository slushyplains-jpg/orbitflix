Norah lets her fiancé dive alone one night, and he never comes back. Now she works at the bottom of the Mariana Trench, where she prefers her glass empty, because there is less to lose. Then something hits the station. Norah shuts a bulkhead door on coworkers screaming to be let in. Every escape pod is already gone. So the captain makes a plan, and it is a mile of walking across the ocean floor before the reactor melts. Rodrigo's helmet is cracked, and he implodes in front of them. Paul, a research assistant in a bathrobe, carries a stuffed bunny named Lil Paul. Something with no eyes follows their lights. In a tight pipe, it drags Paul under the water and rips him out of his suit. Norah is left holding the bunny. The captain tells her to let go, and she says she cannot. Alone, she finds Emily, the biologist, and tells her how she could not find her fiancé. At the last station, a nest of creatures hangs from the ceiling, and Emily's oxygen alarm starts to beep. A creature swallows half of Norah, and she kills it. At the pods, the computer says one is broken. Norah tells them there are three.
