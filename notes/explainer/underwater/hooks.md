# Hook options

1. Norah lets her fiancé dive alone one night, and he never comes back. (wound)
2. Norah tells two survivors there are three escape pods, and the computer has just said one is broken. (the choice)
3. A man in a bathrobe walks across the ocean floor with a stuffed bunny named Lil Paul under his arm. (human detail)
4. The core of the Kepler will melt in thirty minutes, and the only way out is a mile of black seafloor. (clock)

Used as the openings of drafts A, B and C. Final choice is made by the cold read (see cold_read.md).
