# Hook options

1. Norah works at the bottom of the ocean, in a giant underwater base. (plain premise, then the door within two sentences)
2. A woman has to lock a door on her own screaming friends, or the ocean kills everyone. (the choice)
3. A man escapes a sinking underwater base in his bathrobe, holding a stuffed bunny. (wild fact)

Chosen: 1, because it tells a stranger where we are in plain words and gets to the door decision by sentence 3, which is the real hook. Hook 3 is used for the human moment.

Model line: But if she waits, the water floods the whole base, and everyone dies.
