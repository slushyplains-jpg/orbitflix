# Underwater (2020): story bible

Sources: Wikipedia plot summary (paraphrased) and the full subtitle digest. Times are SDH times.

- **Logline:** A mechanical engineer who has been expecting loss ever since she let her fiancé dive alone must cross a mile of black seafloor with the survivors of a destroyed drilling station, hunted by something that has no eyes.
- **Protagonist:** Norah, a mechanical engineer on the Kepler drilling station at the bottom of the Mariana Trench.
- **Wound:** She let her fiancé go diving alone one night because she was tired, and she could not find him. "That feeling of being powerless to change anything. It almost killed me." (1:12:03 to 1:12:53). Since then she keeps her glass empty because there is less to lose (5:31 to 5:51).
- **The choice:** (1) The first hit: she shuts a bulkhead door on crew screaming to be let in (8:41 to 9:13; she says it herself at 19:05). (2) The pod deck: the computer says a pod is not working, and Norah tells the others they have three (1:24:25 to 1:24:50), then forces Emily into a pod because she would give anything for one more second with Sam (1:26:00 to 1:26:32).
- **Human detail:** Paul, a research assistant in a bathrobe, carries a stuffed bunny named Lil Paul (15:12, 32:19). When the creature drags him underwater he shouts for someone to take it (52:11), and Norah later hands it to Smith to give to his family (1:25:21).
- **Goal and stakes:** Reach the Roebuck station, a mile away across the seafloor, before the Kepler's core melts down in about thirty minutes (20:47, 21:14 to 21:42).
- **Central mystery or threat:** What is out there? Eyeless creatures drawn to light, probably woken by the drilling (36:23 to 38:50, 54:00 to 54:26).
- **Key beats:**
  1. Norah's night narration: she prefers her glass empty, there is less to lose (4:47 to 5:51). HIGH.
  2. Something hits the station; Norah shuts the door on the screaming crew (7:10 to 9:13). HIGH.
  3. Every escape pod is gone; the captain stayed behind (17:34, 18:26). LOW.
  4. Thirty minutes to meltdown; the plan is to walk a mile to the Roebuck (20:47 to 21:42). LOW.
  5. Six good helmets are needed; some are ripped (24:20 to 24:50). LOW.
  6. Rodrigo's helmet fails and he implodes; Norah says he knew it was faulty (28:13 to 30:42, 29:48). HIGH.
  7. An imploded pod covered in coils; a creature with no eyes, drawn to their lights (36:23 to 38:50). LOW.
  8. The Kepler explodes behind them (41:54). LOW.
  9. In a tight pipe, something drags Paul underwater and rips him out of his suit; he shouts for someone to take Lil Paul (51:08 to 53:28). HIGH.
  10. Emily says they drilled too deep and the ocean is taking it back (54:00 to 54:26). LOW.
  11. The captain is tangled; he tells Norah to let go and she says she cannot; he dies (57:24 to 1:02:47). HIGH.
  12. Alone at the old station, Norah finds Emily and tells her about her fiancé (1:04:59 to 1:12:53). HIGH.
  13. A nest of creatures hangs from the ceiling; Emily's oxygen alarm wakes them; a creature swallows Norah and she kills it (1:15:18 to 1:21:00). LOW.
  14. At the pod deck the computer says a pod is not operational; Norah says they have three (1:24:25 to 1:24:50). HIGH.
- **Turn:** The pod deck. Norah's lie shows what she has decided.
- **Hold back:** Norah getting Emily and Smith into the pods, overloading the reactor, the survivors, and the credits.
- **Best images:** a man in a bathrobe with a stuffed bunny crossing the ocean floor; the imploded pod wrapped in coils; the eyeless creature in the floodlights; the nest of sleeping creatures hanging overhead; the pod computer saying Pod non-operational.
- **Cliffhanger:** The computer says one pod is broken, and Norah tells them there are three.
