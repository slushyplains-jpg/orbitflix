# Underwater (2020): story bible

Sources: Wikipedia plot summary (paraphrased) and the full subtitle digest. Times are SDH times.

- **Premise:** A woman working in an underwater base at the bottom of the ocean has to walk across the sea floor to another base after hers falls apart, while creatures hunt her crew in the dark.
- **Protagonist:** Norah, a mechanical engineer on the base, who keeps people at a distance because there is less to lose (5:31 to 5:51).
- **Wild facts:** she has to shut a door on her own screaming coworkers to save the base (8:33 to 9:01, 19:05); Paul escapes in his bathrobe and one lucky sock, carrying a stuffed bunny named Lil Paul (15:12, 32:19); a cracked helmet implodes with a man inside it (28:13, 30:42); a creature with no eyes bursts out of a dead body and hunts by their lights (37:44 to 38:50); the last station's ceiling is covered in sleeping creatures (1:15:18 to 1:16:01).
- **Human detail:** Paul's stuffed bunny. When the creature drags him under he screams for someone to take Lil Paul (52:11), and Norah later hands it to Smith for his family (1:25:21).
- **Wound:** she let her fiancé dive alone one night and never found him; the helplessness almost killed her (1:12:03 to 1:12:53).
- **Key beats:**
  1. Walls crack, water pours in; Norah shuts the door on her coworkers (7:10 to 9:13). HIGH.
  2. Every escape pod is gone; the base will blow up in about thirty minutes (17:34, 20:47). LOW.
  3. The plan: diving suits and a mile walk across the sea floor to another base (21:14 to 21:56). LOW.
  4. A cracked helmet implodes on the way down (28:13 to 30:42). HIGH.
  5. A creature bursts out of a dead body; it has no eyes and follows light (37:44 to 38:50). HIGH.
  6. The first base explodes behind them (41:54). LOW.
  7. In a tight flooded tunnel, the creature drags Paul under (51:08 to 53:28). HIGH.
  8. The captain dies saving Norah (57:24 to 1:02:47). HIGH.
  9. Norah finds Emily and Smith; she tells Emily about her fiancé (1:10:11 to 1:12:53). HIGH.
  10. The last base: the ceiling is covered in sleeping creatures (1:15:18 to 1:16:01). HIGH.
  11. Only two of the escape pods work, and there are three of them (1:24:25 to 1:24:40; plot summary). HIGH.
- **Twist we can show:** the last base, their only hope, is a nest of sleeping creatures.
- **Hold back:** Norah's lie about the pods, her staying behind, the explosion she causes, and who survives.
- **Best images:** water bursting through the walls; the door closing on running coworkers; the crew walking in diving suits on the dark sea floor; the creature with no eyes in the flashlight; the sleeping creatures hanging from the ceiling; the escape pod screen.
- **Cliffhanger:** three people, two working escape boats, and Norah has to decide who stays.
