# Spine

(a) Belief: Norah thinks the less you have, the less you can lose, ever since the night she let her fiancé dive alone and could not find him (5:31 to 5:51, 1:12:23 to 1:12:41).
(b) The choice: at the pod deck the computer says Pod non-operational and Norah tells the others they have three (1:24:25 to 1:24:50). Earlier she shuts a door on crew screaming to be let in (8:41 to 9:13).
(c) Cost: held back (it is the ending). The script stops on the lie.
Model line: Luke hid behind a shelf while his best friend was beaten to death, and he did nothing.
