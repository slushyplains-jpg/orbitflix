A man in a bathrobe walks across the ocean floor with a stuffed bunny named Lil Paul under his arm. Paul jokes about everything, even while the walls break. Behind him, a drilling station is falling apart, and the only way out is a mile of black seafloor. Norah, the engineer, has already watched Rodrigo implode in a cracked helmet. Something with no eyes hunts them by their lights. Then comes a pipe so tight they cross it one at a time on a rope. Paul goes last. On the far side, Norah holds the rope. His voice drops out of the radio. They pull, and he comes through grinning, joking about something behind him. His line is tangled, and something pulls him under. He shouts for the others to take Lil Paul. They cannot hold on, and the thing rips Paul out of his suit. All that is left of him is a stuffed bunny, and Norah is holding it. She has lost someone before. One night, she was too tired to dive with her fiancé, so he went alone, and she never found him. Now she walks on alone, with the bunny and a radio nobody answers. She finds Emily and Smith alive, and they reach the last station. At the pod deck, a warning light shows one pod is dead. Norah tells the others there is one for each of them.
