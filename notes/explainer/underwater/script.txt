Norah works on Kepler Station, deep under the sea, where people live for months at a time. A pressure breach wakes the crew, and Rodrigo shouts for everyone to get up. The crew has to seal the bulkhead, or the whole rig will die. Someone is still on the other side of the door, and the crew argues about closing it. Then an explosion shakes the station. The station's warning says structural failure is imminent. Norah asks whether that was an earthquake. Everyone is told to report to the pod bays. The station is seventy percent compromised, and the crew has to move fast. Norah resets the breakers and looks for the nearest pods. Rodrigo says the evacuation pods are in the next section. Paul is hurt, and the crew helps him stand up. Some of the water in the corridor is sewage. What is really happening to Kepler Station, and will anyone reach the pods in time?
