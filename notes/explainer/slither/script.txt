In the small town of Wheelsy, Grant is acting strange, and his wife Starla starts to notice. A girl has disappeared, and the town is already on edge. Starla was getting ready when Grant came home, and his face was already wrong. Starla calls a doctor and insists they need to get him to the hospital. Starla is afraid of what her husband is becoming. The mayor and the chief go after Grant, and the whole town begins to panic. Creatures squeal from the dark, and the crowd starts to scatter. The people who are infected have worms in their brains, and the worms take over their bodies. Some victims are made pregnant, while others are simply taken over. Grant is the center of the whole system, so destroying him might stop the rest. Bill tells Starla to get in the car, and the group races out of the town. Brenda is turned into a worm host, and the others are left shaken. In the end, everything depends on whether anyone can stop Grant. Bill keeps telling everyone to stay together, because the town is falling apart around them. Someone has to stop Grant before the worms reach anyone else.
