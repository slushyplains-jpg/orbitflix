Four friends hike through Sweden, and their friend Rob would have loved this place. Dom leads the group into the forest, and he complains that hiking was easier yesterday. Luke says they were not in the forest yesterday, and the argument starts. Hutch finds a strange shape on a tree. They find an empty cabin, and Hutch says whoever lived there was packing. Phil smashes a chair, and the group is on edge. Luke says the thing in the tree looked like a figure, and Hutch says it could be a spirit. That night, Hutch screams, and Dom wakes to find Gayle is gone. Phil is missing, and the group starts to grab everything and pack in a hurry. Dom says he wants to get out of these woods now. Then something huge roars in the trees. Luke insists he saw something, and Dom will not hear it. Dom has no blood on him, and Luke is not sure what that means. What is out there, and will any of them get out of the forest?
