# The Ritual (2017): story bible

Sources: Wikipedia plot summary (paraphrased) and the full SDH subtitle file. Times are SDH times.

- **Logline:** Haunted by guilt over a friend's death, a man leads three old friends on a shortcut through a Swedish forest, where something ancient starts hunting them.
- **Protagonist:** Luke, the friend who hid while their friend was killed.
- **Wound:** Luke hid behind a shelf during a shop robbery while his best friend was beaten to death (robbery 3:16 to 4:42; hiding from plot summary).
- **Goal and stakes:** Finish the hike in the dead friend's memory and get out of the forest alive. Failing means dying in the woods, and facing what he did.
- **Central mystery or threat:** What is stalking them, and what does the forest want with Luke?
- **Key beats:**
  1. The robbery; the friend refuses to give up his wedding ring and is killed (3:59 to 4:42).
  2. Six months later, four friends hike northern Sweden in his memory (9:48, 10:41).
  3. Dom hurts his knee (12:38; 37:07); they take a shortcut through the forest (13:50).
  4. A gutted elk hung in a tree (18:52; 54:17), symbols carved on trees (22:34).
  5. A storm, an empty cabin (22:45), a twig figure with no head and antlers for hands (25:29 to 25:31).
  6. Nightmares; everyone wakes screaming (30:15 to 30:55); one friend kneels before the figure (31:29; plot summary); Luke has wounds on his chest (plot summary).
  7. They follow a path ("a path means civilisation", 33:11 to 33:37) and find an abandoned tent with shoes left behind (43:45, 44:19).
  8. Luke glimpses something huge (39:55); Dom blames Luke for the robbery (41:11, 41:29).
  9. Hutch is dragged screaming into the dark (49:22 to 49:37).
  10. They decide it followed them from the cabin (53:59).
- **Turn:** Hutch's disappearance; the threat is real and it is following them.
- **Hold back:** Phil's death, the village, the cult and its creature, Dom's sacrifice, Luke's escape.
- **Best images:** the gutted elk in the branches; the twig effigy; the dark cabin; the abandoned tent; the forest at dusk; the robbery in the shop.
- **Cliffhanger:** Hutch is gone, and whatever took him has been following them since the cabin.
